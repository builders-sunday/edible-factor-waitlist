01 / logo

The mark, in Space Grotesk.

One tile: the E and F set in Space Grotesk 700, four periwinkle registration dots, and a single budget orange hairline splitting the letters. It ships in two cuts: a dark cut for dark surfaces and a light cut, its clean inversion, for pale ones. Same geometry, same motion, inverted ink. Reach for whichever holds contrast on the surface you are placing it against.

Dark by default, periwinkle on top.

Periwinkle is the brand hue and carries every accent. Calorie cyan and budget orange are the two meters, one plate read as two numbers. Nothing is pure black or pure white: every neutral is tinted toward the brand. Click any swatch to copy its hex.
