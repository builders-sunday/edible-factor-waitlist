Straight answers before you start

What is Plate?

Plate is the restaurant workspace behind Edible Factor. Your team maintains menu information, manages access, controls the public presentation and prepares printed materials in one place.

Is this the diner app?

Plate is for the restaurant team. The Edible Factor app is what diners use to explore dishes and make their choices. Plate supplies the menu information behind that experience.

Does this replace our POS?

This page describes menu management and the diner experience. It does not promise billing, kitchen routing or a replacement for your POS. Tell us about your existing setup so we can establish the right scope.

What does it cost?

Talk to us about your restaurant and the setup you need. We will confirm current pricing and what is included before you make a decision.

Do you reorder my menu?

No. There is no promoted placement and no sponsored slot. Nobody can pay to appear above you, and dish order is the one you set.

Who owns the listing and the data?

You do. Take it down whenever you want, and your menu leaves with you in a file you can read.

Is the analytics real yet?

The Today and Trends captures contain sample figures while telemetry is connected. Treat them as a product demonstration, not live restaurant performance. We will confirm analytics availability for your setup during the walkthrough.

I already have a menu somewhere. Do I retype it?

Send a CSV, a PDF, or clear photographs of the printed menu. We load it, you check it, and it goes live once you are happy.

What if I have several locations?

Plate supports multiple outlets from the same console. Tell us about your locations so we can walk through the menu, access and publication setup with you.
