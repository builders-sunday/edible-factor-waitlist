Straight answers

The questions you were
going to ask anyway.

What does it cost?

One monthly subscription, and that is the whole bill. No setup fee, no per-outlet charge, no commission on covers, and nothing that appears on month three. Add a second location and the price does not move.

So where is the catch?

There is not one, and that is the point of charging you directly. We are paid by the restaurants who use the console, not by taking a slice of your covers or by selling your placement, which is why nobody can buy their way above you.

Do you reorder my menu?

No. There is no promoted placement and no sponsored slot. Nobody can pay to appear above you, and dish order is the one you set.

Who owns the listing and the data?

You do. Take it down whenever you want, and your menu leaves with you in a file you can read.

Is the analytics real yet?

Not yet, and we would rather you read that here than find out in month two. The dashboard ships today and the screens are real software, but the numbers on Today and Trends are sample values until the telemetry endpoints land. Everything else on this page - the menu, the switches, the listing, Print Studio - is live now.

I already have a menu somewhere. Do I retype it?

No. Send a CSV, a PDF, or clear photographs of the printed menu. We load it, you check it, and it goes live once you are happy.

What if I have twenty locations?

The subscription does not change. Outlets are unlimited on every plan, each with its own hours, covers and switches, all reading one menu underneath.
