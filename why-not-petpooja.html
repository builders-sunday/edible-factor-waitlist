01, the hidden cost

The real Petpooja invoice.

Petpooja lists software at INR 1,500 to 3,500 per outlet per month. That number is what wins the demo. It is not the number you actually pay.

On top of the software fee, Petpooja charges 1.5 to 2 percent on every transaction routed through their billing. The bigger the outlet, the bigger this line item gets, and it never stops growing with you.

05, honest caveats

Where Plate is not the obvious answer.

If we sold you on math alone we would be lying. Three places to be straight about.

  • Small operator

    Single outlet doing under INR 5 lakh per month. Plate is roughly the same all-in price as Petpooja at that scale. Our pitch then is the EF diner channel, not raw cost savings. If you are not interested in the diner side, Petpooja is fine.

  • Maturity gap

    Petpooja has a 15 year head start. They know the Indian restaurant operator daily rhythm in a way we will not match for a few years. Their support staff, their banking integrations, their inventory tooling: real. We are newer.

  • The bet we are making

    We are betting on design, flat pricing, and dual side revenue. If you want a vendor with the most outlets in India today, that is Petpooja. If you want a vendor whose incentives stay aligned with yours as you scale, that is us.
