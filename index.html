01 · The real problem

You've always
had to choose.

Every menu is a silent negotiation between what your body wants, what your wallet allows, and what your week has planned for you. Most apps solve one side of it.

Three reasons why.

Reason · 01

Calorie apps don't know what you ordered. What it cost you.

Reason · 02

Budget apps don't know the receipt total. What it fed you.

Reason · 03

Menus don't tell you whether you can afford the pasta. Calorically, or otherwise.

Ch 01 · Your Home

Dine-in mode,
on the table.

Calories and budget for the session you're sitting in. Set a target, customize a meal, or ask the AI Chat. The home screen tracks your decision in real time, before the bill arrives.

A menu that
reads you back.

The restaurant's full menu, with veg, non-veg, bestseller filters, calorie tags, and allergen badges baked into every dish. Lite mode keeps it fast even on a hotspot.

Tonight's Reasoning explains why the signature cut went first, and offers alternates priced and portioned so the table balances. Tap WHY THIS on any dish for the math.
